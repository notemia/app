Frequently asked questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose ByEar Pro. You can also tap “Manage” on the ByEar home screen.

I bought ByEar Pro but the questions are still limited.

Open ByEar, tap “Go unlimited”, then “Restore purchases”. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

There is no sound.

Check that the volume is up and that the sound is not going to a speaker or headphones you cannot hear. ByEar plays even when the silent switch is on. If it still does not play, close and reopen the app.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Progress and settings are stored only on your device, so deleting the app removes them. Your ByEar Pro subscription is not affected and can be restored.

How do I change the language?

On the ByEar home screen, use the “Language” setting below the list of modules. By default the app follows the language of your iPhone.
