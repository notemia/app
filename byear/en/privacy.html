Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

ByEar is an ear-training app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: ByEar does not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address or location, and it shows no ads.

What the app stores on your device

Your settings and progress (for example the language, difficulty, scores and how many free questions you have used today) are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to ByEar Pro, the purchase is made and billed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details.

To check whether your subscription is active, and to restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created by the app, which is not linked to your name or Apple Account, together with the App Store records of your ByEar purchases (such as which plan you bought and when it renews). This information is used only to provide the subscription. Like any online service, RevenueCat also receives the basic technical data that comes with every request, such as the IP address, device model, system version and app version. RevenueCat privacy policy.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your App Store account settings.

RevenueCat may process this data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law gives you rights over your data (such as access, correction or deletion, or a complaint to your data protection authority), you can exercise them by writing to helpyoutofeel@gmail.com.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No internet connection is needed for practice. The app goes online only to check or buy a subscription.

Children

ByEar is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Write to helpyoutofeel@gmail.com.
