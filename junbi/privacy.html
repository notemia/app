Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy describes how the app Junbi: Olympic Taekwondo (by Notemia) treats your data.

The short version

Junbi does not ask you to create an account. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Notemia does not collect, store or sell any personal data about you.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, chosen fighter, ranking and saved replays are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

The full game

Junbi is free to download; the full game is one in-app purchase. It is made and billed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details: the app only learns from the App Store whether the full game was bought, so it can unlock it and restore it on your other devices.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your ranking points, number of wins and achievements to Apple Game Center, where they appear in its leaderboards and achievements under your Game Center profile. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy; Notemia does not receive your Game Center profile. You can turn Game Center off in the settings of your device.

Fight a Friend (optional)

Online play starts only when you choose it. To connect two players the game uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication and Relay), provided by Unity Technologies. When you use it, Unity creates an anonymous player ID, issues a short join code and processes network data such as your IP address in order to connect you to your friend and pass the match between your devices. Notemia does not receive or store this data. How Unity handles it is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

If you never use Fight a Friend, none of this happens.

Children

Junbi does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The game has no chat, no profiles and no way to share personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: helpyoutofeel@gmail.com.
