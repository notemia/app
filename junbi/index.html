Junbi

Olympic taekwondo

Junbi is a taekwondo game played by the rules of World Taekwondo: electronic scoring, three rounds, fighters from different countries and a realistic kicking technique.

  • Championship, world ranking, training with a coach and a quick single bout
  • Fight a friend online with a short code
  • Fighters from different countries, each with a signature combo
  • No account, no ads, no tracking
