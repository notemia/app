Cento: Guitar Tuner

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-12

Cento does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Microphone

Cento listens to your guitar to tune it and to check your playing. The sound is analysed on your iPhone in real time. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Your songs and files

Songs you paste, audio or video you import and your practice progress are stored only on your iPhone. Delete them in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

No tracking

The app has no third-party code, advertising or analytics. We do not collect, sell or share personal data. The app is suitable for all ages.

Purchase

The optional one-time Pro purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to helpyoutofeel@gmail.com. If the policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
