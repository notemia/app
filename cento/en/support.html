Support

Questions, ideas or a problem? Write to helpyoutofeel@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

The tuner does not hear my guitar

Allow microphone access in Settings › Cento › Microphone. Play one string at a time, close to the phone, in a quiet place.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

On the Practice tab open any Pro tool and tap “Restore purchase”. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I add a song or slow down a recording?

Songbook › Add a song: paste lyrics with chords from any site. Slow-downer › From Files, or share an audio file from another app to Cento.

Is there a subscription?

No. The tuner is free, and Pro is a single one-time purchase.
